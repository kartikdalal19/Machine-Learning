Behind the Algorithm

How CineMatch Works

Your recommendation pipeline combines movie metadata, TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity to identify movies with similar content.

database
Step 01

Feature Fusion

Genres, keywords, tagline, cast and director information are combined into a single content representation.

data_object
Step 02

TF-IDF Vectorization

The combined movie text is transformed into numerical TF-IDF vectors for mathematical comparison.

compare_arrows
Step 03

Similarity Ranking

Cosine similarity compares the selected movie against the catalog and ranks the most similar titles.
